## Mecha Beasts & Vehicles
### Mecha Beasts
The most common mode of transportation, Mecha Beasts are Mechs that mimic a chosen animal and often travel the same way as that animal would. A variety of animals are available, and some might even say that they gain a personality similar to that chosen animal.

### Vehicles
While less used than Mecha Beasts, there are plenty of other modes of transportation in Slugterra. There are Off-Road Cars, Trains, Skateboards, and even a few hover vehicles.
