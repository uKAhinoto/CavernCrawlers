# Languages
There are dozens of languages stretching across the whole of Slugterra, but only a few explicitly mentioned in Cavern Crawlers.
## Terrian
The most common language, spoken by nearly every race out there, with the exception of the Shadow Clan. Most characters know this language as a default.
## Reptilian
Most commonly heard throughout the Jungles, Reptilian is the main language of the Reptiles. Often known for its loose, slithery tone and pronounciation.
## Trollish
A language used by Cave Trolls, mainly in their own homes. Sounds very rocky and rigid, just like their bodies and personalities.
## Molenoid
Written throughout the tunnels and cavern walls, the Molenoid language is used for navigation mainly by the Molenoids themselves, and its verbal sounds like a lot of snorting and disgusting noises.
## Phibish
Spoken underwater, Phibish is the language of Amphibianoids, who use it for intricate discussions when air is not available. It sounds very bubbly due to it being spoken underwater.
## Shadow Speak
The only language the Shadow Clan know, due to the shape of their vocal cords. It is near impossible to understand it without the help of an artifact. Sounds like overlapping whispers and often makes others heads hurt
## Elvish
The language spoken by the Elves, sounding very flowy and elegant, befitting their appearance.
## Darkan
Darkan is primarily spoken by the Dark Bane, sounding very rigid and sharp, hurting those who listen to it.
## Spiderlike
Spiderlike is unique in the fact that it doesn't have a spoken form. Instead they move their body and create vibrations to communicate with others.
