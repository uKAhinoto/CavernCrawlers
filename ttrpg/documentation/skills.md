## Active
Active skills need to be used in order to work and cost Ki.
### Sight Skills
#### Aura Sight
Allows the user to see if 2 slugs are able to do a fusion shot. If so, discovers new fusion shot. Gives disadvantage on attacks while active. Costs "" Ki
#### Curved Sight
Give the user the ability to see around corners, up to half of their sight range. Costs "" Ki
### Blasts
#### Supersonic Shot
Launches the chosen slug out at 1.5x the speed, giving the slug an extra 1D4 of damage. Costs "" Ki
### Effects
#### Pierce
Adds piercing damage to your shot. Roll a d20, if crit, inflict bleeding. Costs "" Ki
