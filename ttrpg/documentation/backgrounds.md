# Backgrounds
Backgrounds give you skills depending on your characters past and also help flesh out your characters story better.
## Slugslinger
Used to the fighting world of Slugterra, you are much better at handling slugs and dodging random shots. You still have a long way to go before being the best though.
### Proficiencies
- Animal Handling
- Athletics
### Reaction Time
+3
## Mechanic
Skilled in the world of engineering, you are able to understand how to fix up your Mechabeast and used to finding the littlest of problems due to the finicky nature of mechanics.
### Proficiencies
- Machinery
- Insight or Perception
### Reaction Time
+1
## Scout
Living life on the edge, you have gotten extremely skilled at quietly mapping out locations and being undetected from even the sharpest of foes.
### Proficiencies
- Stealth
- Nature or Slight of Hand
### Reaction Time
+3
## Performer
Whether you were a dancer, musician, or other form of artist, you are capable of performing out in large crowds and you know exactly how to please the people.
### Proficiencies
- Acrobatics
- Performance
### Reaction Time
+1
## Surface Dweller
How you got to Slugterra may not be clear, but what is, is that you need to hide where you came from at all costs to protect the safety of both worlds. While you are still wet behind the ears to the world of Slugterra, you are well versed in the logic and knowledge of the upper world.
### Proficiencies
- Medicine
- Deception
### Reaction Time
+0
## Civilian
Originally just an ordinary person, for some reason, you have decided to immerse yourself in the world of battling. Due to constantly experience of facing near death every day due to irresponsible battlers, you are skilled in survival.
### Proficiencies
- Survival
- Insight or History
### Reaction Time
+0
## Miner
A job that requires great strength and mental fortitude, you have worked all across the mines in the 99 Caverns, and are trained to deal with quick dangerous circumstances.
### Proficiencies
- Medicine
- Perception
### Reaction Time
+2
## Pirate
A thief and fighter by nature, you used to pillage and steal from anyone who dared enter your territory. Known for your fearsome nature, you are very money oriented and talented in determining if someone is hiding extra loot from you.
### Proficiencies
- Intimidation
- Investigation
### Reaction Time
+2 (Proficient in Water)
