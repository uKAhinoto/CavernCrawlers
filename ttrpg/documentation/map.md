# Map of the 99 Caverns
There are Major Regions in the 99 Caverns. Below are the regions and the caverns that belong to them.

![Mapofthe99Caverns](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/725655fe-a303-4808-a0ea-b10ebd22cfd4)
## Alta Cueva Caverns
The Alta Cueva Caverns are the central region of the 99. Most typical slugs live here.
## Civilized Eastern Caverns
The Civilized Eastern Caverns are located in East. Said to be more wealthy and industrialized than the West.
## The Magma Caverns
The Magma Caverns are a set of magma filled caverns and are home to Magma Monsters, Lava Bats, and Fire Type Slugs.
## The Northern Caverns
The Northern Caverns are also known as the Frozen Northern Caverns and are the northern-most region. Home to slugs who love the cold.
## The Outland Reach
The Outland Reach is a set of unexplored caverns and the Easternmost region in The 99 Caverns. These set of caverns are located between Gateway Cavern and "The End".
## The Shadow Mines
The Shadow Mines are a mysterious and mythical set of caverns in the 99. They are typically only accesible to slugs and the Shadow Clan, but they will make an exception to those who they deem worthy.
## Deep Swamp Caverns
The Deep Swamp Caverns consists of many swampy caverns and toxic slugs.
## The Tropical Caverns
The Tropical Caverns are a set of tropical caverns in the 99 and host many curious slugs, ranging from the Enigmo, to Ancient Slugs.
## Wild Western Caverns
The Wild Western Caverns are the Western-most region of the 99. It's known to be a very dry place.
